## Rationale

**Product fit** (3.0/30): Integration Labs offers 'The Unified API for Business Financial Data.' This suggests a product focused on aggregating or standardizing access to business financial data via an API. However, there is no evidence that it automates a specific, named SMB workflow end-to-end or acts as an agent making decisions or taking actions within a workflow. The offering appears to be an enabling infrastructure/API rather than a workflow-specific agent.

**Team & execution** (7.5/25): The founding team consists of Ishwar Gogineni, Parth Shah, and Sidharth Rao. No further information about their backgrounds, technical expertise, or prior experience is provided in the available data.

**Market & timing** (6.0/20): The product targets business financial data, which is relevant to many SMBs. However, the data does not specify whether SMBs are the primary buyer or if the workflow addressed is common and repeatable across SMBs. The 'Unified API' framing suggests a horizontal tool rather than a vertical, workflow-specific solution.

**Traction** (3.0/15): The only traction evidence available is that Integration Labs participated in YC's Summer 2022 batch. There is no information on user numbers, revenue, funding, or external engagement.

**Differentiation** (1.0/10): There is no information provided about proprietary technology, data, workflow depth, or defensible edge. The product appears to be a unified API, which may face competition from other API aggregation platforms.

**Risks**: Key risks include unclear fit to the thesis due to lack of evidence that the product automates a specific workflow for SMBs or acts as an agent. The team's relevant experience and the product's adoption or differentiation are also not established in the data provided.

## What would change this call

1. Clearer evidence the product owns one named SMB workflow end-to-end (not just assists with it) would raise the score.
2. Surfacing stronger team signal — prior startup/exec experience or a clear technical founder — would raise the score.
3. A more concrete 'why now' (new model capability, regulation, or category shift) or evidence the workflow is common across many SMBs would raise the score.
