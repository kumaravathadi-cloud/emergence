## Rationale

However, it does not automate a specific SMB back-office workflow or act as an agent within such a workflow; rather, it is a tool for founders to find investors.

**Market & timing** (2.0/20): The target market is startup founders seeking investors, not SMBs with back-office or operational workflows. The workflow addressed is investor discovery, which is not a repeatable back-office or ops task for SMBs as defined by the thesis.

**Traction** (3.0/15): The only traction evidence provided is 11 Hacker News points. There is no information on user numbers, revenue, or other adoption metrics.

**Differentiation** (3.0/10): Differentiation is based on a custom embedding model, identity resolution system, and a large, indexed investor database. However, there is no evidence of defensibility in workflow depth, proprietary data, or distribution advantage specific to SMB workflow automation.

**Risks**: The main risk is a lack of fit with the investment thesis: the product does not automate a named SMB workflow or act as an agent within such a workflow. Additionally, there is limited information on team background and no evidence of significant traction beyond HN engagement.
